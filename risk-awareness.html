Actionable Field Guide

The 4 Statutory Stages of PMFBY Risk Coverage

Complete legal protection guaranteed under Ministry of Agriculture guidelines from sowing to post-harvest.

🌱
Stage 1: Pre-Sowing

Prevented Sowing / Planting Risk

In cases where the majority of insured farmers in a notified area intend to sow but are prevented from doing so due to severe deficit rainfall or adverse seasonal conditions.

Benefit: Up to 25% of Sum Insured payable as immediate relief; policy subsequently terminated.
🌾
Stage 2: Mid-Season

Standing Crop Loss (Sowing to Harvest)

Covers non-preventable natural risks: prolonged drought, dry spells, flood, inundation, widespread pest & disease attacks, landslides, natural fire, lightning, storm, and cyclone.

Benefit: Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs) determine shortfall against threshold yield. On-account payment of 25% advance in severe mid-season adversity.
🚜
Stage 3: Post-Harvest

Post-Harvest Losses (Up to 14 Days)

Coverage available for up to a maximum period of 14 days from harvesting for crops kept in "cut and spread" condition in the field for drying against cyclone, cyclonic rains, or unseasonal showers.

Mandate: Farmer must intimate loss within 72 hours for individual farm-level loss survey.
⛈️
Stage 4: Localized Calamities

Localized Disasters (Hailstorm / Landslide)

Loss or damage resulting from the occurrence of identified localized risks affecting isolated plots: hailstorm, landslide, inundation / cloudburst, and natural fire.

Action: Individual assessment conducted by appointed Joint Surveyor & Patwari team within 48 hours of intimation.

PMFBY Coverage & Exclusion Matrix

Clear statutory demarcation under Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare guidelines.

✓ Permissible & Covered Calamities
  • ✅ Hailstorm (ओलावृष्टि): Physical destruction of flowers, foliage, and standing grains.
  • ✅ Inundation / Flooding: Standing water causing root rot and lodging.
  • ✅ Post-Harvest Rains: Up to 14 days when cut crop is spread in the field for drying.
  • ✅ Drought & Dry Spells: Widespread monsoon failure verified by CCEs.
  • ✅ Pest & Disease Epidemics: Uncontrollable blight, bollworm, or locust swarms.
  • ✅ Natural Fire & Lightning: Accidental fire caused by natural elements.
✕ Ineligible & Excluded Perils
  • ❌ War & Civil Strife: Damage arising from armed conflict or riot.
  • ❌ Nuclear / Radiation: Contamination from radioactive waste or weapons.
  • ❌ Malicious Destruction: Intentional destruction of standing crops by humans.
  • ❌ Theft & Stolen Produce: Theft of harvested grain from field or storage.
  • ❌ Animal / Cattle Grazing: Destruction by domestic livestock or stray animals.
  • ❌ Preventable Agronomic Neglect: Refusal to spray recommended pesticides or weeds.
