⚖️ Important Statutory Protocol: Guidance vs. Authorized Channels

BhoomiBandhu provides end-to-end guidance and generates an official PMFBY Emergency Loss Intimation Dossier & Reference Number (`BB-CLM-2026-XXXXX`).

In accordance with Ministry of Agriculture operational guidelines, localized calamity claims require formal acknowledgment by authorized state channels:

1. Central Helpline:
Call 14447 toll-free and quote your BhoomiBandhu Claim ID.
2. Revenue Patwari:
Present printed Form 1 Receipt to your local Halqa Patwari.
3. Official Portal:
File or cross-verify directly on pmfby.gov.in.
4. Bank / CSC Branch:
Inform the bank branch that debited your crop insurance premium.
