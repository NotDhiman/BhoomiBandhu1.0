Official PMFBY Premium Guidelines & Government Subsidies

Under PMFBY, the farmer pays only a small fraction of the actuarial premium. The rest is fully co-funded by the Central and State Governments.

Crop Category Applicable Season Farmer Premium Share Govt Subsidy Share Perils Covered
Food Grains, Pulses & Oilseeds Kharif (Monsoon) 2.0% of Sum Insured Balance (up to 98%) paid by Govt Drought, Flood, Pests, Inundation
Wheat, Mustard, Barley, Gram Rabi (Winter) 1.5% of Sum Insured Balance (up to 98.5%) paid by Govt Frost, Hailstorm, Unseasonal Rain
Annual Commercial & Horticultural Crops Both Kharif & Rabi 5.0% of Sum Insured Balance paid by Govt Severe weather & localized calamities
