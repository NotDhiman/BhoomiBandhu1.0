Understanding Farming Pressure & Emotional Well-Being

Farming is one of the most physically and emotionally demanding occupations in the country. Unseasonal rain, drought, pest attacks, debt pressure, and mandi price swings can create acute mental exhaustion and despair.

Feeling overwhelmed, anxious, or unable to sleep is not a personal failure. Reaching out for medical or psychological support is a courageous and practical step to protect yourself and your family. Under the National Mental Health Programme (NMHP), every farmer has a statutory right to free, confidential counseling and psychiatric care at government District Hospitals and through national helplines.

📜 Statutory Financial Protection & Relief for Farmers in Distress

Debt anxiety is a leading contributor to farmer distress. You have statutory legal rights under Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines and state agricultural moratorium frameworks:

1. Natural Calamity Restructuring

When an area is declared calamity-affected, short-term crop loans (KCC) can be converted into term loans repayable over 3 to 5 years, with up to a 1-year moratorium.

2. Ban on Coercive Recovery

RBI circulars strictly prohibit banks and recovery agents from using intimidation, abusive language, or calling between 7:00 PM and 7:00 AM.

3. PMFBY Immediate Relief

File a crop claim on BhoomiBandhu within 72 hours of damage to receive up to 25% on-account interim payment under PMFBY guidelines.
