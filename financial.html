2. Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Operational Guidelines

The Government of India's flagship credit scheme providing revolving agricultural working capital at just 4% interest.

Apply on JanSamarth Portal →
🎯
Pillar 1 • Subvention

Effective 4% Interest

RBI sets nominal baseline rate at 7.0%. Central Govt provides a 3.0% Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI) upon timely repayment within 1 year, making the net interest rate only 4% p.a.

⚖️
Pillar 2 • Security

₹1.60 Lakh Collateral Free

Under RBI guidelines, banks cannot demand land mortgage or collateral security for KCC limits up to ₹1.60 Lakh (extendable up to ₹2.00 Lakh for tie-up arrangements).

📐
Pillar 3 • Limit Metric

Scale of Finance (DLTC)

District Level Committee calculates: Crop cultivation expenses + 10% post-harvest/household + 20% farm maintenance. Approximately ₹45,000 to ₹55,000 per acre.

🔄
Pillar 4 • Flexibility

5-Year Revolving Facility

KCC card is valid for 5 years with an automatic 10% annual limit step-up to counter input price inflation. Draw funds via RuPay KCC ATM card whenever needed.

🐄 Extended to Animal Husbandry, Dairy & Fisheries:
Farmers without cultivable land can also obtain KCC up to ₹2 Lakh at 4% interest for cattle feed, veterinary expenses, and livestock shed operations.

4. Statutory Eligibility & Mandatory Documents

Verify that you satisfy bank underwriting guidelines before submitting your loan application.

👤 Borrower Eligibility Criteria

  • ✓ Land Category: Individual owner cultivators, joint borrowers, tenant farmers, oral lessees, sharecroppers, and SHGs/JLGs.
  • ✓ Age Requirement: Minimum 18 years, maximum 75 years (Co-borrower mandatory if age > 60).
  • ✓ Credit Record (CIBIL): No active wilful defaults with any commercial or cooperative bank.
  • ✓ Sowing Verification: Must have cultivable agricultural land or active livestock farming in district.

📋 Mandatory Document Dossier Checklist

  • 1. Identity & Address Proof: Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or PAN Card.
  • 2. Land Records: Latest Record of Rights (RoR / Khasra-Khatauni / Patta copy).
  • 3. Crop Sowing Certificate: Patwari declaration or certified sowing receipt.
  • 4. Bank Account Passbook: 6 months statement with Aadhaar DBT linkage.
  • 5. No Dues / Self-Declaration: Affidavit stating no outstanding default loans.
