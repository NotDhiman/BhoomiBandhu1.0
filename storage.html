🏛 Government Storage Subsidies & Eligibility Guidelines

Official schemes under Ministry of Agriculture and Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI) supporting farmer storage.

1. Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAMPADA Yojana (PMKSY) – Cold Chain Subsidy

Statutory Benefit: 35% tariff concession for general areas and 50% for North-East/Himalayan states on cold chain tariffs.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Individual farmers, FPOs, and cooperatives storing perishable horticulture crops (potatoes, onions, fruits).
  • Storage facility must be accredited by MoFPI or National Cold Chain Development board.
  • Direct credit transfer or automatic deduction from warehouse invoice.

2. Gramin Bhandaran Yojana / Agricultural Marketing Infrastructure (ISAM / AMI)

Statutory Benefit: 25% to 33.33% storage rent and capital subsidy on WDRA registered rural godowns.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Small & Marginal farmers, Women farmers, and SC/ST farmers receive 33.33% subsidy tier.
  • General farmers receive 25% subsidy tier up to statutory caps.
  • Applies to dry grain warehouses, rural godowns, and grain silos.

3. Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF) Storage Subvention

Statutory Benefit: 3% per annum interest subvention on post-harvest infrastructure loans + 20% storage concession credit.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • All farmers holding verified land records (RoR / Khasra-Khatauni).
  • Produce stored in WDRA registered warehouses with electronic warehouse receipts (e-NWR).

4. Electronic Negotiable Warehouse Receipts (e-NWR) Pledge Loan

Statutory Benefit: Avail bank loan up to 75% of produce value at 7% subsidized interest rate without distress sale.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Valid deposit in any WDRA registered warehouse listed on BhoomiBandhu.
  • Instant issuance of e-NWR on farmer's registered mobile number.
