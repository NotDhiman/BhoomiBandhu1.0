Popular Farmer Welfare Schemes

PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

₹6000 annual income support for eligible farmer families, credited directly to bank accounts.

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana

Crop insurance coverage against losses due to natural calamities, pests, and diseases.

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Easy access to short-term agricultural loans at subsidized interest rates.

Gramin Bhandaran Yojana

Subsidy support for rural storage infrastructure and warehouse development.
